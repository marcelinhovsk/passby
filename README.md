PassBy ♿

Your city. Your perspective. Your change.

PassBy is a community-driven web project focused on improving urban accessibility in Leiria, Portugal. It encourages citizens to identify and report problems affecting pedestrian crossings and accessible mobility, helping the community understand where urban infrastructure needs improvement.

🌍 About the Project

Urban accessibility plays an essential role in creating inclusive cities. PassBy aims to give people a simple way to highlight accessibility issues in their surroundings and contribute to a more inclusive urban environment.

Through an interactive map and community reports, PassBy brings visibility to pedestrian crossing problems and encourages collective action towards better urban mobility.

✨ Features

Accessibility Reports: Identify and report issues affecting pedestrian crossings and pedestrian accessibility.

Map of Reports: Explore reported locations and discover accessibility concerns across Leiria.

Community Impact: Display the total number of reports and the most frequently reported issue.

Issue Categories: Organize reports by accessibility, infrastructure condition, signage, and other problems.

Responsive Web Interface: Navigate the platform through a clear and accessible website.

Community Engagement: Connect users with the project and its social media channels.
